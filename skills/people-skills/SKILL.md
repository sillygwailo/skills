# People Skills

**Goal: While avoiding manipulation, further my goals and be able to sleep at night by being kind and emotionally resilient at the same time.**

People are complicated, and deserve to be treated the way they want to be treated (within reason, and while maintaining one's own energy levels). There are a lot of ways to get what I want and be able to sleep at night.

## 1. Understand first

- Try to get a sense of what a person is like. It's difficult to know what they want or what they're like by how they look, so overcome that as quickly as possible.
Read the affected code, conventions, dependency manifest, and tests.
- Find out something about them that I didn't know before. Even in small talk, maybe I can learn something.

## 2. Consider what I can bring to the table

1. **Use humour:** It's a gift I bring to the world. I can make most people laugh fairly easily.
2. **Find common ground:** What do we agree on?
3. **Maintain eye contact:** People have a whole body, but you're talking to them through their eyes.
4. **Spend energy on having my outside reflect my inside emotions:** Empathy is a two-way street. If I want to know how people feel, they need to know how I feel.

Pick the option that meets the requirement with the least owned complexity. Do not stop at a rung that only nearly fits.

**Tie-breakers, in order** (when options both meet the requirement):
1. Less code, fewer dependencies, fewer concepts for the project to own.
2. Easier to reverse or remove.
3. More conventional for this codebase and ecosystem.

**Exceptions:** Exit a conversation with jerks as soon as possible. There's not enough time on this planet for cruelty.

## 3. Depth

See how deep the conversation can go. Do I allow myself to be vulnerable? Only if I sense I get that from the person I'm talking to.

## 4. Boundaries

- I'm probably not going to touch you in the early stages of a relationship.
- I probably need to know if you're partnered within the first 7 minutes of meeting you. These days, it's only to satisfy my curiosity, but I'm dying to know almost right away.
- If you don't know who I am and I don't know who you are, could we try radical translucensy right of the bad? What are we going to do, doxx each other?
